पावन घाट

〰

Har ghat ki apni rooh hai. Kisi bhi card pe click karo aur Panditji se detail mein poochho! Dashashwamedh pe aarti, Manikarnika pe antim sanskar, Assi pe musicians aur chai — Kashi ke ghats ek poori duniya hain.

Dashashwamedh Ghat

दशाश्वमेध घाट

Kashi ka dil. Yahan Brahma ji ne Dashashwamedha Yagna kiya tha. Har roz sham 7 baje — 5 purohit, 5 diyas, aasman tak uthti aag. Ye dekhna aur ye mehsoos karna — alag hi anubhav hai.

⏰ 24hr · Aarti: 6:45 PM
→

Manikarnika Ghat

मणिकर्णिका घाट

Mahashmashaan — Mrityu ka Ghat. 3,500 saal se har roz yahan chitak jalti hai. Shiv khud Taraka mantra sunate hain yahan marne walon ko. Life ka most intense darshan milta hai yahaan.

⏰ 24hr (No Photos!)
→

Assi Ghat

अस्सी घाट

BHU students, musicians, sadhus, aur backpackers ka adda. Sham ko Subah Aarti se shuru hoti hai ek extra Aarti, phir chai, sitar, tabla. Tulsidas ji ne yahan kavitaein likhi thi.

⏰ Subah 5 AM · Sham 6 PM
→

Kedar Ghat

केदार घाट

Bengali community ka ghat — red-white horizontal stripes famous hain. Kedarnath Jyotirlinga ka ek roop yahan bhi hai. Subah ki pooja yahaan bahut peaceful hoti hai.

⏰ Subah 5–8 AM
→

Scindia Ghat

सिंधिया घाट

Iconic doobe hue Shiv mandir ke saath. 1830 mein Gwalior ke Scindia family ne banwaya. Mandir itna bhaari tha ki Ganga mein dabb gaya — ab sirf chhat dikhti hai paani ke oopar. Photography goldmine!

⏰ Sunrise Best
→

Panchganga Ghat

पञ्चगंगा घाट

5 rivers ka mythical sangam — Ganga, Yamuna, Saraswati, Dhutapapa, Kirana. Aurangzeb ki alamgir masjid yahan se dikhti hai. Dev Deepawali pe yahan 80,000 diyas jalaye jaate hain.

⏰ Dev Deepawali Must
→

The Ganga Boat Ride

नाव की सवारी

Ghat nahi, experience hai. Subah Assi se Manikarnika tak ki nau-sawari — dhund, ghanti, dua, diyas — Varanasi ki asli rooh yahaan hai.

⏰ 5–7 AM | ₹150–300/hr

6:45 PM (Garmi) · 5:45 PM (Sardi)
Grand spectacle! 5 senior purohit ek saath, vishaal multi-tiered agni diyas, shankh, dhol, mantra jo aasman tak uthte hain. Ganga mein aag ka pratibimb koi camera nahi pakad sakta.
Raat ki Ceremony
Shayana Aarti
10:30 – 11:00 PM

Ghat ke steps pe front seats 30 min mein bhar jaate hain. Paani ke upar conch shells ki awaz — wahi asli cheez hai.

Tourist ki Guide

ज़रूरी सुझाव

📋
✈️
Kaise Pahunchen
  • Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS) — flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata
  • Train: Varanasi Jn (BSB) ya Manduadih (MUV) — Delhi se 10-12 ghante Vande Bharat
  • Lucknow se road: 3.5 ghante, Prayagraj se: 2 ghante
  • Airport se ghats tak: ₹400–600 Ola/Uber ya ₹300–400 auto
🏨
Kahan Thakein
  • Budget: Zostel, Stops Hostel (Assi area, ₹500–1200)
  • Mid-range: Hotel Clarks Varanasi, Ramada (₹2000–5000)
  • Luxury: BrijRama Palace, Taj Gateway (₹8000+)
  • Pro tip: Ghat-view room ke liye BrijRama ek heritage palace hai — Darbhanga Ghat pe
🛡️
Tourist Traps — Bacho!
  • Fake priests jo ghat pe welcome karein aur puja karwate hue paisa maangein
  • Boat wale jo overcharge karein — ₹150-300/hr hai sahi rate, ₹1000 nahi
  • Silk shops jo synthetic ko genuine Banarasi bolke bechein — BFC shop pe jaao
  • Manikarnika pe photo ke liye paisa maangne wale — bilkul mat do
🕐
Timing Secrets
  • Subah 5 AM pe Ganga — foggy, mystical, almost no tourists
  • Vishwanath darshan: 3 AM Mangala Aarti ya 6-8 PM — kam bheed
  • Evening Aarti: 60 min pehle pahuncho, front seats jaldi bhar jaate hain
  • Malaiyo sirf 7-10 AM milti hai, Nov-Feb mein — miss mat karna!
🗺️
Day Trips
  • Sarnath: 10 km, Buddha ka pehla pravachan, Dhamek Stupa — ₹150-200 auto
  • Ramnagar Fort: 14 km, Kashi Naresh ka palace — ₹100 auto
  • Vindhyachal: 75 km, Vindhyavasini Devi — half day trip
  • Chunar Fort: 40 km, river cruise option bhi hai
📱
Emergency Numbers

  • Ambulance: 108
